Most companies bleed thousands of hours and tens of thousands of dollars paying staff to manually read PDFs, copy-paste rows between spreadsheets, update CRM fields, and clean up outdated vendor or inventory catalogs. Your Company AI Brain turns database creation and ongoing maintenance into a 100% autonomous pipeline.

CAPABILITY 01

Have 10,000 legacy PDFs, vendor price lists, scanned invoices, old spreadsheets, email threads, or call recordings? Whenever a supplier emails a new price sheet, a client submits an intake form, a technician logs a voice note from a job site, or a webhook fires from Stripe/QuickBooks, your AI Brain automatically parses the change, matches the existing record, updates the exact rows across all connected databases, and logs a timestamped audit trail.

CAPABILITY 03

"Command Your Database" in Plain English

Stop writing complex queries or clicking through 500 records one by one. Tell your Company AI Brain: "Ingest this 80-page Q4 supplier catalog, update all unit costs in our database, recalculate gross margins, and flag any item that dropped below 30% margin." What used to take an analyst 3 days finishes in 12 seconds.

CAPABILITY 04

Self-Healing Data Hygiene, Deduplication & Web Enrichment

Your AI Brain continuously scans your large databases for duplicate accounts, missing phone numbers, expired insurance certificates, or conflicting pricing—automatically merging duplicates, enriching missing company data via APIs, and alerting your team only when human approval is needed.
